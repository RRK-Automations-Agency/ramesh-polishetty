Common questions

Questions about eating plans

Short answers to what people ask before starting. Anything else, ask on the contact page.

Do I have to weigh every meal?

No. You get a simple daily structure of what to eat and roughly how much — no weighing every gram.

Will I have to give up the food I normally eat?

No. Plans are built around the food you already like, with simple local swaps, so the plan is one you can keep following after the first month.

Is nutrition coaching only for fat loss?

No. There are three directions — fat loss, muscle gain and balanced maintenance. The structure changes with the goal; the approach does not.

Can I still eat out or travel?

Yes. Eating out without falling off track is part of the balanced lifestyle plan, and the fat-loss and muscle-gain plans are adjusted whenever your week changes.

How often does the plan change?

Weekly check-ins keep it honest: you share photos or notes and the plan is adjusted from what they show.

Do I need the training side as well?

Nutrition works alongside personal training, but it does not have to start at the same time. The programs page explains how the two fit together.
