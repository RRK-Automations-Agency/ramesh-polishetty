Common questions

Before you start

The questions people ask most before a first session. Anything not covered here, ask directly on the contact page.

Do I need experience to start?

No. Every program opens with an assessment of your body, your schedule and your goal — the plan is built from what that shows, not from a template.

Can I train online instead of in person?

Yes. Online coaching is the same structured training and nutrition work — plan, check-ins and adjustments — delivered to your own gym, wherever you train.

How do check-ins work?

You share progress — photos, measurements, strength numbers or notes — and the plan is adjusted from what those reviews show. The nutrition plans run on weekly check-ins.

Which program should I pick?

Start from your goal, not the label. Fat loss usually combines nutrition coaching with 1-on-1 training; strength goals lean on the powerlifting work.

Can I see results before starting?

Yes — the transformations gallery shows client photos and the client reviews page has what those clients said about the process.

How much does coaching cost?

Prices are not published on this site. Plans are quoted individually after the free consultation, so you are quoted for what you actually need rather than a package.
