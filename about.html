The Coach

Who Ramesh Polisetty is, how he coaches, and what you can expect when you train with him.

Complete fitness coaching, built on honesty

Ramesh Polisetty is a complete fitness trainer focused on body transformation. He doesn't chase trends and he doesn't overpromise — he builds a plan around your body type, your daily routine and your long-term goal, then coaches you through it one step at a time.

From the first consultation to the final check-in, the journey is structured, measurable and honest. Training plans, nutrition guidance and progress tracking work together as one system — so the results you earn are results you keep.
