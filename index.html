Where Ramesh coaches

In person around Medchal and Hyderabad — or online, anywhere

Ramesh Polisetty coaches clients in person around Medchal and the wider Hyderabad area, in Telangana, and coaches online clients wherever they train. It is the same programme either way — assessment first, then structured training, nutrition guidance and regular check-ins — the difference is only how the sessions are delivered.

This is a service-area business, so there is no public address to visit: Ramesh comes to the coaching arrangement rather than the other way round. If you are outside Hyderabad, online coaching travels better — the programs page explains what each path includes, and the contact page is where to ask which one fits.
